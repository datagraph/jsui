Overview

This chapter tells you how to read your account information, edit your account metadata, and manage your authentication token.

Read Your Account Information

When you sign in, the application opens your account page. It shows:

  • Account friendly ID (username)
  • Email address
  • Full name
  • Homepage URL
  • Blog URL
  • Company name
  • Location
  • List of repositories

Edit Your Account Metadata

Edit the profile fields to keep your account information current.

Fields You Can Edit

Edit any of these:

Field Description
firstname Your first name
familyname Your family/last name
fullname Your full name
email Your email address
homepage URL to your personal homepage
blog URL to your blog
company Your company name
location Your location
phone Your phone number
skype_id Your Skype ID
jabber_id Your Jabber/XMPP ID
workinfo Work-related information

How to Edit a Field

Step 1: Open your account page: click your account name in the navigation, or go to the account directly.

Figure 1: Account metadata field in edit mode with new value entered

Step 2: Find the field you want to edit in the account information section.
Step 3: Double-click the field value. The field turns into an input box.
Step 4: Type the new value, or change what is there.
Step 5: Press Enter, or click outside the field. The field goes back to displaying its value, now the one you typed.
Step 6: Click Save, in the tab bar, to write your changes to the server.

Figure 2: Account page showing the Save button in the tab bar

Note: the application tracks only the fields you touched. Save sends those fields, not the whole account configuration.
Important: nothing reaches the server until you click Save. Leave the page without saving and you lose your changes.

Find Your Authentication Token

Your account page shows your authentication token. Use it to reach the Dydra API from a program.

Read the Token

  1. Open your account page
  2. Find the "Token" section, in the sidebar or the account information area
  3. Read your current token there

Reset the Token

To replace your token with a new one:

  1. Open your account page
  2. Find the token section
  3. Click Reset Token, where the button appears
  4. The service issues a new token and shows it
  5. Update every application or script that used the old one

Figure 3: Account page showing token section with Reset Token button

Security Warning: guard your token. Whoever holds it holds your account. Reset it at once if you suspect anyone else has seen it.

Move Around From the Account Page

From your account page you can:

  • See all your repositories
  • Create a repository
  • Open any one of them
  • Reach the repository management controls

Work With Several Accounts

Hold several accounts open at once, and:

  • Switch between them
  • Work with repositories drawn from more than one
  • Keep accounts that carry different permissions
Note: each account holds its own session and its own credentials. Keep as many account tabs open as you like.

When Something Goes Wrong

A Change Does Not Stick

If an edit to your account metadata does not survive:

  • Check that you clicked Save after making it
  • Look for an error message — it names the problem
  • Check that you may edit the account
  • Check that your network reaches the service

The Token Does Not Appear

If you cannot see your token:

  • Check that you signed in to the right account
  • Check that you may read tokens
  • Reload the page
